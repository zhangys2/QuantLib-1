# Day-category weighting

Type: Grilling (HITL)

## Question
Should the abstraction support weights by day category, optionally use a `Calendar` for holiday classification, and give holiday weight precedence over weekday weight?

## Resolution
Yes. Support day-category weights with optional holiday classification from `Calendar`; a holiday weight overrides the weekday weight.
